Frequently asked questions

Why is there no sound?

Check the device volume and silent mode, then tap the speaker button again.

How is progress saved?

Progress is saved automatically on your device. No account is required.

How do I erase my learning data?

Open Settings in the app and choose Reset Learning Progress. You can also delete the app to remove all local data.

中文帮助

为什么没有声音？

请检查设备音量和静音模式，然后再次点击扬声器按钮。

学习进度如何保存？

进度会自动保存在你的设备上，无需注册账户。

如何删除学习数据？

打开 App 内的“设置”，选择“重置学习记录”；也可以删除 App 来清除所有本地数据。
