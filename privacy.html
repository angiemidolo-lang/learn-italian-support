English

Learn Italian is designed to work without an account. The current version does not collect, sell, or transmit personal information, advertising identifiers, or learning records to the developer or a third-party server.

Data stored on your device

Course progress, quiz scores, streaks, mistakes, favorites, daily goals, and language settings are stored locally on your device using Apple’s system storage.

Device features

The app uses Apple’s on-device speech synthesis to pronounce Italian words and phrases. It does not request access to your microphone, location, photos, camera, or contacts.

Deleting data

You can reset learning progress from Settings at any time. Deleting the app removes all data stored locally by the app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes

If app features or data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information on the support page.
